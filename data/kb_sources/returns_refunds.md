
## General Policy
Because our products are perishable food items, we are unable to accept returns once
a box has been opened or the seal has been broken. This policy exists to protect food
safety and product quality for all customers.

Unopened, sealed items may be returned within 3 days of delivery for a full refund,
provided the packaging is intact and the item has been stored appropriately (cool, dry
place, away from direct sunlight).

## Damaged or Incorrect Orders
If your order arrives damaged, incorrect, or missing items, please contact us within
24 hours of delivery with photos of the issue. We will offer either a full replacement
at no additional cost or a full refund, your choice.

Claims submitted after 24 hours of delivery may not be eligible for replacement, since
we're unable to verify the condition of perishable goods after that window.

## Refund Method and Timeline
Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method used at checkout. Refunds
typically appear within 5–10 business days, depending on your bank or card issuer's
processing time. We do not offer cash refunds or store credit unless specifically
requested and agreed to in advance.

## Custom and Gift Boxes
Custom-built date boxes (plate builder orders) are made to order and are therefore
final sale — we're unable to accept returns or offer refunds on custom boxes unless
the order arrives damaged or does not match what was confirmed at checkout.

## How to Request a Return or Refund
Contact our customer service team through the website contact form or via the chatbot,
providing your order number and a description of the issue. Our team will respond
within 1 business day with next steps.
