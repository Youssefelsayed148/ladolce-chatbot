## Current Discount Policy

We do not run a recurring or seasonal percentage-off discount program, and we
don't issue promo codes or maintain a loyalty/points scheme at this time. The
prices shown in the chatbot and on our product catalog are our standard
prices — there is no separate "sale" pricing to ask about.

## Corporate, Bulk, and Wholesale Orders

Guests placing a large order — corporate gifting, event favors, or bulk
quantities beyond a typical individual order — are welcome to reach out
directly so our team can put together a custom quote. This is handled
case-by-case rather than through a fixed published discount tier, since
pricing for a large order depends on quantity, product mix, and timeline.
Please contact us through the website contact form or via the chatbot with
the occasion, approximate quantity, and desired delivery date, and our team
will follow up with options and pricing.

## Corporate Gifting Without a Custom Quote

If you don't need a bespoke bulk arrangement, our existing Gift Boxes & Trays
line (including our Small, Medium, and Large trays) and the custom
box/plate builder both work well for corporate or group gifting at standard
pricing, without needing to go through a separate quote process — the
chatbot can help you build one directly.

## Free Add-Ons

Gift wrapping with an optional personalized note is available as a free
add-on at checkout on any order — see our shipping and packaging policy for
details. This isn't a discount, but it's worth mentioning to anyone asking
what's included at no extra cost.
