## Allergen Handling

Our kitchen prepares dates alongside a wide range of nuts, dairy, and chocolate
products, and does not currently operate a dedicated allergen-free production
line. Tree nuts (almond, pistachio, cashew, hazelnut, pecan), sesame (used in
our tahina-filled dates and pastes), dairy (milk and white chocolate coatings,
cream fillings), and gluten/wheat and soy (biscuit and chocolate-coating
ingredients in some stuffed dates) are all present in our kitchen and may
cross-contact any product made there — including items that don't list any of
these as a primary ingredient.

If you or a guest you're ordering for has a severe or life-threatening
allergy, please contact our team directly before ordering rather than relying
on a product name or description alone — we can talk through the specific
item you're considering, but we do not currently issue a certified
allergen-free guarantee for any product.

## Reading Our Product Names

We name products after their visible ingredients (e.g. "Khudri Date with
Pistachio," "Sukkari Date Milk Chocolate With Nuts"), so the name and
description are usually a reliable first signal of what's inside — a product
named "... with Cashew" contains cashew, "Milk Chocolate" or "White Chocolate"
means a dairy-based coating, "With Tahina" means sesame, and so on. When
asked about a specific product's ingredients, it's reasonable to reason from
what the name/description already says, while being clear that this is a
best-effort read of the listed ingredients, not a formal allergen statement —
for anything allergy-critical, the guest should confirm directly with our
team.

## Vegan and Dairy-Free

We do not currently hold a formal vegan certification, and we don't maintain
a labeled vegan/non-vegan flag for every item. Plain, unstuffed dates contain
no animal products. Beyond that, it varies by product: anything described as
"milk chocolate" or "white chocolate" coated, or with a "cream" filling, is
very likely dairy-based and not vegan, while a product whose name lists only
dates and a plant ingredient (a nut, seed, or fruit) with no chocolate or
cream mentioned is a plausibly vegan option worth asking about — but "cream"
fillings in particular can be dairy- or nut-based depending on the recipe, so
please confirm the specific product with our team rather than assuming, if
you're ordering for a strictly vegan guest.

## Other Dietary Questions

For any dietary requirement not covered above (halal, kosher, or a specific
ingredient not mentioned in a product's name or description), please ask our
team directly rather than assuming — we'd rather confirm than have a guest
guess wrong on something that matters to them.
