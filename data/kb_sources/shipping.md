## Domestic Delivery
La Dolce Saveur ships to all major cities within the country via our courier partners.
Standard delivery takes 2–4 business days from order confirmation. Express delivery
(next-day) is available for orders placed before 12:00 PM local time, subject to a
surcharge shown at checkout.

Free standard shipping applies to all orders over 800 EGP. Orders below this threshold
carry a flat shipping fee of 60 EGP.

## International Shipping
We currently ship internationally to the GCC countries (UAE, Saudi Arabia, Kuwait,
Qatar, Bahrain, Oman) with delivery times of 5–8 business days. International orders
may be subject to customs duties and import taxes in the destination country, which
are the responsibility of the customer and are not included in the displayed price.

We do not currently ship to Europe, North America, or Asia. Customers in these regions
are welcome to contact us directly to discuss special arrangements for large or
corporate orders.

## Packaging
All dates and stuffed date products are packed in temperature-controlled, food-safe
packaging designed to preserve freshness during transit. Gift boxes and trays include
protective inner padding to prevent shifting during shipping.

Gift wrapping is available as a free add-on at checkout — select "Gift Wrap" and add
an optional personalized note, which will be printed on a card and included in the box.

## Order Cutoff Times
- Same-day delivery (select cities only): order before 10:00 AM
- Next-day express: order before 12:00 PM
- Standard delivery: no cutoff, processed within 1 business day of order confirmation

Orders placed on public holidays will begin processing on the next business day.
